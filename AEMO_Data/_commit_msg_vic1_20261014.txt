Carry the predictor source and the VIC1 windows as the tracked data set

The repository held five regions of every window while this project has only ever
scored VIC1, and the predictor tree sat where it had been built rather than in one
place. This commit is the two things that belong in the remote: the predictor, and
the two organised AEMO windows.

Predicitor/  84 .m and one README, 0.4 MB, nothing else. 44 of the files were not
  tracked under this path before, because the tree had been built under
  Weekly/EMPC/2026-09-23/predictor/. 8 more were modified in place. This is the
  whole predictor: the registry and the shared axis, the sixteen models plus the
  four that are registered but not atlas columns, the ensemble runs, and the
  figure and report scripts.

AEMO_Data/window_120d_20260201_20260601_vic1/  2026-02-01 .. 2026-06-01, 120 days.
  p5min_5min_VIC1.csv 414,433 rows, predispatch_30min_VIC1.csv 319,681 rows,
  dispatch_5min_VIC1.csv 34,539 rows.

AEMO_Data/window_60d_20260811_20261009_vic1/  2026-08-11 .. 2026-10-09, 60 days.
  predispatch_30min_VIC1.csv 159,841 rows, pd7day_30min_VIC1.csv 63,241 rows, and
  p5min_vic1_partial/ with the 5-minute set and the two dispatch extracts.

Both directories are renamed to say they hold VIC1 only, and each README carries
a note at the top saying so. The row and size tables inside them still describe the
five-region set they were written for, and the note says which line applies.
Restoring five regions is a re-run of the build scripts the READMEs name.

The other four regions are deleted from the working tree rather than kept: five
regions is 575 MB against 133 MB, and both windows are regenerated from NEMWEB.

AEMO_Data/level_multiwindow_figs/ is deliberately NOT tracked. It holds seven
.mat error atlases of 283 to 468 MB, above the 100 MB per-file limit, and every
one of them is rebuilt by Predicitor/run_full_ensemble_test.m. Its eight small
PNGs are outputs of the same runs and are also left out.

The 45 tracked files under the old AEMO_Data/ paths resolve as 16 renames (VIC1
files that kept their contents), 11 renames into AEMO_Data/samples/, and 18
deletions (the four non-VIC1 regions of each set). The VIC1 monthly price series
is completed to 14 files; eight of them were already tracked and six were added.

Timestamps in every file are NEM time, AEST UTC+10, with no suffix. Prices are
unclamped and unfilled. Nothing in this commit changes a number: the CSVs that
were already tracked are byte-identical, which the R100 similarity in the rename
detection confirms.
